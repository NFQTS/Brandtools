OUR PURPOSE

A safety net for the age of intelligence

AI will create extraordinary abundance — but only if we deliberately soften the transition for those whose livelihoods are disrupted.

Preserve Employment

Direct grants and low-interest capital to businesses and workers adapting to AI — keeping people in meaningful roles during transition.

Future-Proof Skills

Fund world-class retraining programs so humans remain valuable partners to intelligent systems rather than being displaced by them.

Dignity First

Emergency support for families facing sudden disruption — because no one should lose their sense of purpose while the world evolves.
